# General Championship

## Open Soft 2025-2026

## Introduction

An event where participants will be provided with a problem statement containing specifications of the software to be developed within the stipulated time.

## Procedure

Any member or representative of the team needs to do the registration for the entire team.

Submitted Teams will be informed through mail for their presentation slot.

Later on, participants need to provide a well-defined report. Following that, teams have to present their presentation.(depends on company)

## Deliverables

AS MENTIONED IN THE PROBLEM STATEMENT

1. Source Code: Fully containerized. A single docker-compose up command must launch the backend engine, the market generator, the trading bots(optional), and the frontend terminal simultaneously.
2. Architecture & Quant Report: A document (max 10 pages) containing:
   - System architecture diagram mapping the data flow from the Matching Engine to the Frontend.
   - The mathematical logic/parameters used for your Synthetic Market Generator and Trading Bots.( This point is optional)
3. Presentation explaining the solution (max 15 slides): 10% weightage

## Timeline

- Release of Problem Statement – 13th March 2026
- Deliverables Submission – 3rd April 2026| 01:00:00 pm
- Presentation – 3rd April 2026

## General Rules

- A maximum of 20 participants per hall is allowed and a maximum of 4 of them are free to speak at the time of presentation.
- The evaluation by judges will be on the basis of both the Presentation and the Software.
- Judges’ decisions are final and binding for all participants.
- Communication with judges in the course of the event is not allowed. If any hall violates this rule, 10% of the total points will be deducted. More than once if contacted then that hall will be disqualified from the event.
- Each team will be assigned a Team code. Teams may use only the Team code to identify themselves in the submitted report and Presentation. Any other identification mark, if detected, will lead to the disqualification of the team.
- .All presenters of the team allotted the ’(n + 2)’ th slot must be physically present at the venue of the event, within five minutes of the completion of presentation and question answer session of the team allotted the ’n’ th slot. Failure to do so will lead to a deduction of 15% of the total marks obtained by the team.

If the team is not present within 5 minutes of the completion of the presentation and question answer session of the team allotted the ‘(n-1)’th slot, then the ‘n’ th team will be disqualified.

The criteria for evaluation will be revealed by the judge(s)

ON THE BASIS OF PROBLEM STATEMENT

- Backend & Architecture (20%): Accuracy of the matching engine, WebSocket stability, and ability to handle high message throughput without crashing.
- Frontend & UX (50%): Responsiveness of the web terminal, quality of the charting, and intuitive UI/UX design.
- Quant & Bot Logic (5% Bonus): The realism of the synthetic market data generator and the logical soundness/profitability of the automated trading bots.
- Code Quality & Deployment (20%): Clean, modular code, proper Dockerization, and seamless 1-click execution.
- Presentation explaining the solution (max 15 slides): 10% weightage

The Median Marking rule will be applicable.

In case of any discrepancy, reference shall be made to the soft copy.

The Submissions, after being collected, will be enclosed and sealed with signatures of one representative from each participating hall.

- The Standard libraries/API used should be mentioned during the presentation. No penalties will be applicable on this.

Any given quantifiable specification in the problem statement, if exceeded will result in the following penalty:

- if exceeded by upto 10%, then 10% of the marks obtained by the team in that section is deducted.
- if exceeded by up to 20%, then 20% of the marks obtained by the team in that section is deducted.
- if exceeded by more than 20%, then the team is awarded no marks in that section.

If ‘n’ is the number of times this rule is violated, when
